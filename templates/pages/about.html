What we do

We put modern vending machines in offices, warehouses, gyms, apartment buildings and coworking spaces, then take care of everything after that: stocking, restocking, cleaning, repairs and payments. The host pays nothing. We earn from product sales, so a machine only works for us if it works for the people using it.

Every machine is cashless and AI-powered, with live inventory so we restock before items run out. Most machines also have a built-in advertising panel. For larger sites we also run micro markets.

Where we work

We're based in Fremont and work across the Bay Area, including Fremont, Newark, Union City, Milpitas, San Jose, Palo Alto and Menlo Park. We assess each location individually. What matters is consistent daily traffic, not the size of your team or space.
