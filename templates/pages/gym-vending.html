What gym members actually buy

We've learned what sells in fitness environments and what sits untouched. The winners: protein bars (RXBAR, Quest, Built), electrolyte drinks (LMNT packets, BodyArmor, Liquid IV), sparkling water (Topo Chico, Waterloo), protein shakes (Muscle Milk, Premier Protein for post-workout convenience), and energy drinks for pre-workout demand.

The conventional approach — loading a gym machine with the same chips and candy bars as an office break room — consistently underperforms. Members who just finished a workout aren't looking for that. A curated, performance-forward mix drives more purchases and better member satisfaction than a generic lineup.

What we stock for fitness environments

  • Protein: RXBAR, Quest Bar, Built Bar, Larabar, protein shakes (Muscle Milk, Premier Protein), beef jerky
  • Electrolytes & hydration: BodyArmor, Liquid IV, LMNT stick packs, Gatorade, coconut water
  • Energy: Celsius, Ghost Energy, Reign, Reign Storm (lower sugar), Alani Nu
  • Sparkling water: Topo Chico, Liquid Death, Waterloo, Spindrift
  • Recovery-friendly snacks: mixed nuts, peanut butter packets, dark chocolate, dried fruit

Gym types we serve

fitness_center

Independent & boutique gyms

Smaller gyms can generate consistent vending volume if they have 100+ active members. We work with independent operators who want to offer a member perk without adding inventory management overhead.

apartment

Residential fitness centers

Apartment and condo fitness rooms are a high-potential placement. Residents using the building gym late in the evening or early morning represent consistent, recurring demand that a lobby machine alone doesn't capture.

corporate_fare

Corporate fitness facilities

Companies with on-site gyms often have vending coverage in the main break room but nothing near the fitness area. Adding a performance-focused machine near the gym serves a distinct employee need.

sports

Multi-sport facilities

Facilities with basketball courts, pools, climbing walls, or other multi-sport offerings see sustained usage throughout the day from different athletic populations. Broader product mix, higher volume.

Frequently asked questions

add

For gyms that want to subsidize member pricing as part of their membership value, we can explore discounted price points. This typically requires a minimum size threshold. Get in touch to discuss what works for your membership base and business model.

What about refrigerated products like protein shakes?
add

Our standard machines handle ambient (non-refrigerated) products. For cold protein shakes and refrigerated items, a combination of a standard snack machine and a cold beverage machine covers both temperature categories. We'll assess your space and recommend the right configuration.
