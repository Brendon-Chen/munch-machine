Why Menlo Park offices care about the details

In a firm where partners host founders and lab managers host collaborators, the shared kitchen gets seen. A machine that's half-empty, cash-only, or stocked with the same five snacks as a gas station sends a quieter signal than anyone intends. A clean, well-stocked, cashless machine with a thoughtful mix sends the opposite one.

The Bayfront area adds a practical reason too: many life sciences and office buildings there are a drive from anywhere to eat. For lab teams running experiments on long timelines, on-site food and drinks save real time — and our live inventory monitoring means the machine is restocked before anyone notices a gap.
