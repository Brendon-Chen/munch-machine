Why Mountain View offices care about break room quality

Mountain View's tech community has a particular relationship with wellness. The proximity to Caltrain, the outdoor culture of the mid-Peninsula, and the demographics of the tech workforce here combine to create a workplace where what's stocked in the vending machine is genuinely noticed. Not just as a perk — as a statement about what the company values.

We build Mountain View product mixes with this in mind. Organic and clean-label brands get stronger representation. Low-sugar and plant-based options are core, not afterthoughts. Premium sparkling water and cold-brew coffee anchor the beverage selection. And the classics are still there — balance, not restriction — because people buy snacks for pleasure as well as fuel.

What we stock for Mountain View workforces

  • Snacks: RXBAR, Kind, Larabar, organic trail mix, roasted chickpeas, rice cakes, dark chocolate, lightly salted nuts
  • Beverages: Topo Chico, Liquid Death, Waterloo, cold brew (Chameleon, High Brew), Organic Valley shakes, coconut water
  • Alongside classics: Kettle chips, Clif bars, Gatorade, standard sodas — because real product mix data shows people want both
