Why Palo Alto offices care about what's on-site

Downtown Palo Alto has plenty of cafés, but Stanford Research Park and the office buildings off Page Mill Road and East Bayshore don't. For a lot of Palo Alto teams, the nearest decent coffee or snack is a drive, not a walk — and in a startup or lab running on tight timelines, that's a real cost repeated several times a day.

On-site vending closes that gap without adding a vendor your operations person has to manage. We own the machine, stock it based on live sales data, and handle every repair. For a ten-person startup in a shared building or a research facility running late shifts, that means food and drinks are there when people need them, with zero overhead.

What we stock for Palo Alto workplaces

  • Snacks: RXBAR, Kind, protein chips, organic trail mix, roasted seaweed, dark chocolate, lightly salted nuts
  • Beverages: Cold brew, sparkling water, kombucha, protein shakes, coconut water, energy drinks with clean ingredient labels
  • Alongside classics: Kettle chips, Clif bars, Gatorade, standard sodas — because sales data consistently shows people want both

Frequently asked questions

Do you serve small startups in shared Palo Alto buildings?
add

Yes. In multi-tenant buildings, a machine in the lobby or shared break room serves every company in the building, which works well for smaller teams. The arrangement can be with the building manager or with one tenant on behalf of the floor.

Can you serve research and lab facilities with after-hours staff?
add

Yes. Machines run 24/7 with cashless payment, so late-shift researchers and weekend staff have the same access as the daytime team. Live inventory monitoring means we restock based on actual use, including off-hours demand.

Can we request mostly healthy options?
add

Yes. We can weight the mix heavily toward organic, low-sugar, and high-protein products, and adjust it over time based on what your team actually buys and requests.
