Vending in Sunnyvale's competitive talent market

Sunnyvale companies compete for engineering and technical talent against some of the most well-resourced employers in the world. The workplace experience — including break room quality — is part of that competitive landscape in a way it simply isn't in most other markets.

Managed vending isn't a cafeteria replacement, but it closes a meaningful gap for companies that want to signal investment in their team without the overhead of a full food program. A well-curated micro market or premium vending setup tells employees something about how the company operates — and that signal matters during onboarding, during performance reviews, and during the moments when employees are deciding whether to stay.

Premium product curation for Sunnyvale: In this market, we lean heavily toward quality — organic brands, protein-focused options, premium cold brew and sparkling water, international specialty items. The standard candy-bar-heavy mix doesn't reflect Sunnyvale workforces and we don't stock it as a default here.

Services available in Sunnyvale

For smaller Sunnyvale offices (30–80 people), a dedicated snack and beverage machine pair with a premium product mix is the typical starting point. For larger campuses and companies with 50 or more employees in a single building, a micro market is often a stronger fit — open shelves, fresh food, self-checkout, and the kind of variety that replaces off-site lunch runs.

All Sunnyvale placements include cashless payment (Apple Pay, Google Pay, tap-to-pay) as standard, real-time inventory monitoring, and proactive restocking before machines run low.
