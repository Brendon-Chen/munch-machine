Serving Union City's commercial community

Union City's commercial footprint is concentrated but active — the Alvarado-Niles corridor, the industrial areas near the BART station, the retail and office developments around Decoto Road, and the logistics operations that take advantage of the city's central location between major East Bay markets. The workforce here is diverse, transit-connected, and increasingly tech-adjacent as development around the BART station continues.

For Union City businesses managing break rooms, the challenge is typically the same regardless of industry: nobody has bandwidth to order snacks, manage a cash box, and coordinate with a vending company's service schedule. We eliminate that entirely — one call to set up, and then it simply works.

The proximity advantage: Union City is literally on our way. Our Fremont-based routes pass through Union City regularly, which means faster response times and more flexible service scheduling than operators based in Oakland or San Jose.

What Union City businesses typically need

The most common placements in Union City are:

  • Warehouse and light industrial break rooms — High-energy snacks, beverages including sports drinks and energy drinks, and calorie-dense options for physically demanding shifts
  • Office and commercial break rooms — Standard snack/beverage mix with cashless payment; product mix adjusted for office culture vs. industrial workforce
  • Retail staff areas — Combo units or smaller machines for back-of-house staff break rooms in retail environments along the commercial corridors
  • Residential amenity spaces — 24/7 accessible vending for apartment communities near the BART station and along Mission Boulevard
